Single-device locking (A5)
A voucher locks to the first device that uses it and refuses every other device. Phones that rotate their MAC address (iOS "Private Wi-Fi Address", Android "Randomized MAC") can lock out the customer who paid — use Reset Lock to free the code for their device. Repeated resets on one voucher usually mean the code is being shared.
